Why we made Chess Buddies

Chess Buddies began as a father's gift to his son. Tej loved chess, but the chess apps he found spoke only English, and he studies in Gujarati. So his father built him one on his phone, at night and during bus rides. Tej loved it. Now it's for every child who loves chess.

What's inside

♟ 9 friendly bots

From Pip the baby chick to Robo GM. Each has a personality and the challenge grows step by step.

🗺 Adventure map

Lessons from Pawn Village to Endgame Mountain: pieces, rules, checkmates, tactics, openings and endgames.

🧠 2,600+ puzzles

Daily Puzzle, Puzzle Rush and 18 themes, with your own puzzle rating.

🎮 Mini-games

Pawn Wars, Knight's Journey, Capture Chain and Square Finder.

🇮🇳 Chaturanga

The original Indian chess, born 1,500 years ago, with its own story and computer opponent.

⭐ Game review

A coach shows the best moves and mistakes after each game. Plus an analysis board.

Made for families

👧 Up to 6 children

Each child has their own progress, rating and trophies on one phone.

🔐 Parent area

Daily play-time limit, optional parent PIN and a gentle daily puzzle reminder.

📊 Weekly report

See what your child learned and share a progress card with family.

🛡 Child-safe

No accounts, no chat, no personalised ads. Purchases need a grown-up check.
